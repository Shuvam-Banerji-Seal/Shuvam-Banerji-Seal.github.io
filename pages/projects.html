Molecule3D: LAMMPS in the browser

Molecular dynamics has a steep first step: an input script in a language of its own, a build with the right packages, and a trajectory you cannot see. Molecule3D, also called the LAMMPS Web GUI, puts all of it in a web page. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded.

Open the app Source Read the article

TypeScript, React, three.js, WebGL and WebGPU · ★ 36 · created 2025

  • MD notebook. LAMMPS input runs in the page, on the GPU through WebGPU or on CPU threads. 702 of the 894 scripts in the LAMMPS examples tree run, and the engine is checked against native LAMMPS on 2,736 parity cases.
  • Script builder. A flowchart over 185 LAMMPS commands that imports existing in.* files and checks a script against rules that each cite their docs.lammps.org page.
  • Compiler helper. 46 CMake options and 71 packages become a build script for native LAMMPS.
  • Structure viewer. LAMMPS data and dump files, XYZ, PDB and CIF, with playback, measurements, RDF and MSD analysis.
  • Licence and tests. More than 3,000 tests. Free for education and non-commercial research under a source-available licence since 22 September 2026, and earlier releases were MIT.
