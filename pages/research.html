Research

Most of my published work is in information retrieval and the evaluation of language models: putting the right evidence in front of a person or a model, and measuring when the model ignores it. In chemistry I work computationally, with density functional theory on catalysts and molecular dynamics for simulation. This page lists the papers, thesis projects, datasets, talks and service in full.

Research experience

  • July 2025

    Hybrid RAG with automated source citations

    With Dr. Dwaipayan Roy, IISER Kolkata

    A retrieval-augmented generation pipeline over IISER Kolkata intranet documents. Documents are collected with Selenium and read with PyMuPDF and EasyOCR, then chunked with metadata and embedded with Qwen3-4B into a FAISS index. Dense retrieval and BM25 are fused with reciprocal rank fusion, queries are refined with hypothetical answers, and a Streamlit app shows answers with inline citations. The method is explained in an article on hybrid search.

  • 2024

    A retrieval model for TREC tip-of-the-tongue queries

    Guided by Dr. Dwaipayan Roy, IISER Kolkata. Paper listed above.

    A multi-layer BM25 filter in Lucene (Java) that narrows the search domain from extracted keywords, with query expansion and semantic matching by local language models using multi-shot and chain-of-thought prompting. Its results were on par with models modified specifically for dense passage retrieval.

  • May to July 2025

    Research intern, HistoXai

    Astroloop Technologies Pvt. Ltd., Bangalore

    A comparison of tools that assess the quality of digital histopathology slides, reviewing more than 30 open-source frameworks (among them HistoQC, PathProfiler, GrandQC, HistoROI and FASTPathology) for architecture, datasets, performance metrics and limits, to inform later model integration. Work was in Python with OpenCV, PyTorch, scikit-learn and pandas.
