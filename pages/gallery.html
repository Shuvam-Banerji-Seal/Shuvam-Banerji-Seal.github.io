Figures drawn live in your browser by the same code as the homepage. Drag any of them to rotate, or focus one and use the arrow keys.

Each orbital is 1,600 Monte Carlo samples of |ψ|²: red and blue mark the two signs of the wavefunction. The radius is drawn exactly from its Gamma distribution and the direction is rejection-sampled from the angular part. Source: orbital.js.
