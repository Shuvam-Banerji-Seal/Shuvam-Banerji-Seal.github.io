Research

Most of my published work is in information retrieval: getting the right evidence in front of a person or a language model. In chemistry I work computationally, with DFT on catalysts and molecular dynamics.

    ECIR 2026. First author, with Aheli Poddar, Alok Mishra and Dwaipayan Roy. A configurable retrieval-augmented pipeline for agricultural questions, released with a 15,247-entry dataset.

A molecular viewer and LAMMPS workbench that runs on the GPU in your browser. Open LAMMPS data, XYZ, PDB or CIF files, assemble input scripts from 186 commands, and play back dump trajectories.
