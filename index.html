Research

Most of my published work is in information retrieval: putting the right evidence in front of a person or a language model, and measuring when the model ignores it. In chemistry I work computationally, with DFT on catalysts and molecular dynamics.

  1. 2026

    AgriIR: a scalable framework for domain-specific knowledge retrieval

    ECIR 2026 Shuvam Banerji Seal, Aheli Poddar, Alok Mishra, Dwaipayan Roy

    A configurable six-stage retrieval-augmented generation pipeline for agricultural questions, with deterministic citations, released with a 15,247-entry open dataset.

    Paper arXiv Code Dataset

  2. 2026

    SycoLex: a cross-jurisdictional benchmark for detecting sycophancy in legal case reasoning

    CIKM 2026, under review Shuvam Banerji Seal and collaborators

    1,954 cases from the US Supreme Court, the Supreme Court of India and Indian consumer courts, with six sycophancy-inducing prompts, five models and expert annotations. It is the data behind the FIRE 2026 shared task I co-organise.

    Dataset Shared task

  3. 2025

    Hierarchical opinion classification using large language models

    FIRE 2025 Shuvam Banerji Seal, Alok Mishra, Utkarsha Ghosh

    Parameter-efficient fine-tuning of Gemma with a two-layer classification head and a class-weighted loss, after recasting three-level opinion labels as eight flat classes.

    Paper Code

  4. 2024

    IISERK@ToT_2024: query reformulation and layered retrieval for tip-of-tongue items

    TREC 2024 Shuvam Banerji Seal and Subinay Adhikary (joint first), Soumyadeep Sar, Dwaipayan Roy

    Finding a book or film from a half-remembered description: four-step query reformulation with a language model, then two layers of BM25, for a recall@1000 of 0.8067.

    Paper Code Write-up

  5. In prep.

    Computational modelling of [VO(SALIEP)(DTP)] as a water-reducing catalyst

    Manuscript Shuvam Banerji Seal, with Soumyajit Roy

    A DFT study (B3LYP, Gaussian) of a vanadium complex: orbital energies, electronic structure and the reaction pathway for water reduction.

Molecule3D: LAMMPS in your browser

Molecular dynamics has a steep first step: an input script in a language of its own, a build with the right packages, and a trajectory you cannot see. Molecule3D, the LAMMPS Web GUI, puts all of it in a web page. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded.

  • MD notebook. Type LAMMPS input into cells and run it on your GPU or CPU threads. 702 of the 894 scripts in LAMMPS's examples/ tree run, checked against native LAMMPS on 2,736 parity cases.
  • Script builder. Build an input script as a flowchart from 185 commands, import an existing in.* file, and check it against rules that cite the LAMMPS docs.
  • Compiler helper. 46 CMake options and 71 packages turned into a build script you can run.
  • Viewer and analysis. Data, dump, XYZ, PDB and CIF files on the GPU, with playback, measurements, RDF, MSD and density profiles.

TypeScript, React, three.js, WebGL and WebGPU. 3,000+ tests. Free for education and non-commercial research.
